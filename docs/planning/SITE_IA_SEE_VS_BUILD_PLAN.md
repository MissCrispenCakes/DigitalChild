# Finding your way around GRIMdata {#site-ia-plan-diataxis-everywhere-see-vs-build}

GRIMdata brings the research projects, evidence tools and documentation together.

## Main entrances

- [Home](../index.md): the research programme and published work.
- [Explore](../explore/index.md): country assessments, maps, comparisons and downloads.
- [Projects](../projects/index.md): established studies and future investigations.
- [Documentation](../docs/index.md): instructions, methods and technical reference.
- [About](../website/index.md): research contributions, history and context.

## Documentation

The documentation follows [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr): **Tutorials** support learning through a guided activity; **How-to guides** help complete a task; **Reference** describes interfaces and formats; **Explanation** develops the reasoning behind the methods.

Project pages link to their methods and tools. Research history, context and data governance are also available through About.
